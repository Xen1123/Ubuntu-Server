<h1 align=center>Immich Images</h1>

## What is Immich?

- Immich is the self hostable equivalent to Google Photos. It is private, fast, and familiar. It has a very similar UI to Google Photos and functions very similarly (aside from selling your data, of course!)
    - [Immich Official Site](https://immich.app)

## How do I install Immich?

- To setup the Immich server, see [Immich.sh](./Immich.sh)
    - To install Immich on your Mobile device, see the following.
        - On Android, you can find it on the Play Store, F-Droid, or just download the APK [here](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/download/v3.1.0/app-arm64-v8a-release.apk)!
        - On iOS, download it on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/immich/id1613945652)
